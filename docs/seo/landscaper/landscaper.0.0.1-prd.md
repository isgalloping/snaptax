12. 重点分类说明
12.1 Plants & Job Materials
Plants, trees, shrubs, sod, soil, mulch, gravel, edging, irrigation parts, and other materials purchased for customer projects may qualify as business expenses.

记录建议：

Add the customer, project, or service address when materials relate to a specific job.

Job Note示例：

Front-yard planting — Oak Street
Mulch installation — Customer 204
12.2 Tools & Equipment
Landscapers may purchase mowers, trimmers, blowers, chainsaws, spreaders, hand tools, pressure washers, and other equipment used in the business.

风险提示：

The tax treatment may depend on the equipment’s cost, useful life, and business-use percentage. Larger equipment may need to be depreciated.

页面不得将所有大型设备描述为可以立即全额扣除。

12.3 Fuel & Lubricants
Fuel, two-cycle mix, equipment oil, grease, and other operating fluids may be business expenses when used for landscaping equipment.

记录建议：

Add a note identifying whether the purchase was for equipment, a work truck, or mixed business use.

产品可使用备注模板：

Fuel for mower and trimmer
Fuel for work truck
12.4 Repairs & Maintenance
Replacement blades, belts, filters, spark plugs, tires, sharpening, servicing, and equipment repairs may qualify as business expenses.

记录建议：

Include the equipment name or asset in the receipt note.

示例：

Commercial mower — blade and belt replacement
12.5 Vehicle and Trailer Expenses
Travel between customers, suppliers, nurseries, equipment dealers, and disposal facilities may create eligible business vehicle expenses.

必须说明：

普通通勤与业务行驶需要区分
个人与业务使用需要区分
用户需要保留日期、距离、目的地和用途

Use SnapTax to organize fuel, parking, toll, maintenance, trailer, and other vehicle-related receipts. Keep a separate mileage log when needed.

Fertilizer, herbicide, pesticide, weed-control products, and related application supplies may be business expenses when purchased for landscaping work.

16. FAQ模块
FAQ 1：What can landscapers deduct on their taxes?
Potential business expenses may include qualifying plants, materials, tools, equipment, fuel, repairs, vehicle costs, rentals, safety gear, licenses, insurance, disposal fees, advertising, and professional services.
FAQ 2：Can landscapers write off equipment?
Equipment purchased for landscaping work may qualify as a business expense. Tax treatment may depend on its cost, expected useful life, and business-use percentage.
FAQ 3：Can I deduct mower and equipment fuel?
Fuel and operating fluids used for landscaping equipment may qualify as business expenses when properly documented and separated from personal use.
FAQ 4：Can landscaping materials be deducted?
Plants, trees, soil, mulch, gravel, irrigation parts, and other materials purchased for customer projects may qualify when supported by appropriate records.
FAQ 5：Can I deduct my landscaping truck and trailer?
Eligible business vehicle and trailer expenses may be deductible, but personal commuting and business travel must be distinguished.
FAQ 6：Can landscapers deduct equipment repairs?
Routine servicing, replacement parts, blade sharpening, and qualifying equipment repairs may be business expenses when related to landscaping work.
FAQ 7：Can I deduct dump fees?
Green-waste disposal, debris hauling, and related fees may qualify when connected to landscaping business activity.
FAQ 8：Does SnapTax track mileage?
SnapTax can organize vehicle-related receipts, but it does not currently replace a complete mileage log.
FAQ 9：Does SnapTax file taxes?
No. SnapTax organizes receipts and expenses and creates tax-ready reports. It does not prepare or file tax returns.
FAQ 10：Can I export a report for my accountant?
Yes. You can export an organized expense report and use it when reviewing your records with an accountant or tax preparer.
