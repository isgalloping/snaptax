1. 项目背景

SnapTax 是一款面向美国独立承包商、1099 工作者和小型企业的极简费用管理工具，核心能力包括：

拍照保存业务收据
自动识别商户、金额和日期
根据业务用途整理费用
追踪年度业务支出
汇总税务季所需数据
导出 tax-ready expense report
无需强制注册即可开始使用
支持移动端和 PWA 使用场景

HVAC 从业者在日常工作中会产生大量高频、分散的小额业务费用，例如工具、零件、制冷剂、安全装备、车辆、培训和许可证费用。这些费用通常散落在卡车、工具箱、钱包、邮箱及供应商账户中。

税务季到来时，HVAC 承包商需要重新查找和整理这些凭证，容易出现：

收据遗失或褪色
忘记某项费用的业务用途
个人费用与业务费用混合
不清楚哪些支出可能属于业务扣除
需要手工整理银行账单和纸质收据
向会计师提供的数据不完整
年底集中整理耗费大量时间

因此，HVAC 行业与 SnapTax 的“拍照保存、自动整理、税季导出”能力具有较高匹配度。

9.5 重点扣除分类详细说明
9.5.1 Tools and Equipment

建议文案：

HVAC technicians often purchase specialized tools such as manifold gauges, multimeters, drills, vacuum pumps, recovery machines, leak detectors, ladders, and diagnostic equipment.

记录提示：

Keep the receipt, purchase date, cost, and a short note explaining how the tool is used in your HVAC work.

风险提示：

The tax treatment of an item can depend on its cost, useful life, and business use. Some equipment may need to be depreciated rather than deducted immediately.
9.5.2 Parts, Materials and Supplies

建议文案：

Filters, fittings, wiring, capacitors, fasteners, insulation, sealants, cleaning products, and other job supplies may qualify as business expenses when purchased for HVAC work.

记录提示：

Add the customer, project, or job type in the receipt note when possible.
9.5.3 Vehicle and Mileage Expenses

建议文案：

Driving between customer locations, suppliers, job sites, and other business destinations may create eligible vehicle expenses.

注意事项：

通勤与业务里程需要区分
不得暗示家庭到固定工作地点的通勤一定可扣除
SnapTax 当前主要记录车辆相关收据
如果产品尚未提供完整 mileage tracker，不得宣称自动追踪里程

建议产品文案：

Use SnapTax to organize fuel, parking, toll, maintenance, and other vehicle-related receipts. Keep a separate mileage log if you plan to use the standard mileage method.
9.5.4 Training, Certifications and Licenses

建议文案：

HVAC professionals may pay for EPA certification, license renewals, code training, safety courses, manufacturer training, and continuing education.

记录提示：

Save the payment receipt together with the course, certification, or license name.
9.5.5 Safety Gear and Work Clothing

建议文案：

Protective equipment required for HVAC work may include gloves, eye protection, respirators, hearing protection, hard hats, and specialized protective clothing.

注意事项：

普通日常服装不应被描述为必然可扣除。

9.5.6 Phone, Software and Business Services

建议文案：

HVAC contractors often use phones, scheduling tools, invoicing apps, cloud storage, websites, and other services to operate the business.

记录提示：

When an expense has both personal and business use, record the business-use portion and keep a note explaining the allocation.

9.11 FAQ 模块
FAQ 1：What can HVAC technicians deduct on their taxes?
Potential business expenses may include qualifying tools, equipment, parts, supplies, safety gear, licenses, training, insurance, business mileage, software, advertising, and professional services. Deductibility depends on the nature of the expense, its business use, and the taxpayer’s circumstances.
FAQ 2：Can HVAC tools be written off?
Tools purchased for HVAC work may qualify as business expenses. The tax treatment may depend on the cost, expected useful life, and business-use percentage of the tool.
FAQ 3：Can I deduct my HVAC service truck expenses?
Business vehicle expenses may be deductible, but personal commuting and business travel must be distinguished. Taxpayers generally need records supporting the business use of the vehicle.
FAQ 4：Can HVAC technicians deduct work clothes?
Specialized protective clothing or safety equipment required for HVAC work may qualify. Ordinary clothing that can be worn outside work is generally treated differently.
FAQ 5：Can I deduct EPA 608 certification costs?
Certification, renewal, and training expenses related to maintaining or improving skills used in an existing HVAC business may qualify, depending on the circumstances.
FAQ 6：Do I need to keep every HVAC receipt?
Keeping receipts and supporting records helps document the amount, date, vendor, and business purpose of an expense. Additional records may be needed for vehicle, travel, equipment, and mixed-use expenses.
FAQ 7：Does SnapTax file taxes for HVAC contractors?
No. SnapTax organizes receipts and expenses and creates tax-ready reports. It does not prepare or file tax returns and does not replace a qualified tax professional.
FAQ 8：Does SnapTax automatically track mileage?

如果尚无里程追踪功能：

SnapTax can help organize vehicle-related receipts, but it does not currently replace a complete mileage log. Keep a separate record of the date, destination, distance, and business purpose of each trip.
FAQ 9：Can I give my SnapTax report to my accountant?
Yes. You can export an organized expense report and use it when reviewing your records with an accountant or tax preparer.
FAQ 10：Is SnapTax a full accounting system?
No. SnapTax is intentionally focused on receipt capture, expense organization, and tax-season reporting for independent workers and small businesses.

28. 最终产品原则

HVAC 页面不是一篇单纯罗列扣除项目的税务文章，而是一条完整的 SEO 转化路径：

搜索 HVAC 税务问题
→ 看到与工作场景高度相关的内容
→ 理解应及时保存收据和业务用途
→ 认识 SnapTax 的极简记录方式
→ 开始拍摄第一张收据
→ 持续积累结构化费用记录
→ 税务季导出报告

页面应始终围绕 SnapTax 的核心优势展开：

不做复杂会计系统，不替用户报税，而是帮助 HVAC 承包商在费用发生时保存证据、整理记录，并在税务季前生成更清晰的费用报告。
