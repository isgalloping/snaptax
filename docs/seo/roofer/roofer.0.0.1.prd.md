1. 重点费用分类说明

10.1 Roofing Materials & Supplies

建议文案：

Roofing materials may include shingles, underlayment, flashing, fasteners, sealants, membranes, coatings, vents, drip edge, and other supplies purchased for roofing work.

记录建议：

Add the customer, project, or job address when materials relate to a specific roof.

Job Note示例：

Asphalt shingle replacement — Oak Street
Commercial roof repair — Project 204
10.2 Tools & Equipment

建议文案：

Roofers may purchase nail guns, compressors, saws, drills, moisture meters, hand tools, generators, and other equipment used in roofing work.

记录建议：

Keep the receipt, purchase date, cost, and a note explaining the business use.

风险提示：

The tax treatment may depend on the equipment’s cost, useful life, and business-use percentage. Some equipment may need to be depreciated rather than deducted immediately.
10.3 Safety Equipment

建议文案：

Roofing safety expenses may include harnesses, anchors, lifelines, helmets, gloves, eye protection, respirators, warning systems, and other fall-protection equipment.

记录建议：

Keep receipts for the original purchase, replacement components, inspection, and maintenance when relevant.

不得宣称所有普通服装都可以扣除。

10.4 Ladders, Scaffolding and Access Equipment

建议文案：

Roofing contractors may purchase or rent ladders, scaffolding, platforms, lifts, roof brackets, and other access equipment.

风险提示：

购买与租赁可能使用不同税务处理方式
高价值设备可能涉及折旧
页面不直接判断用户应采用哪种处理方式
10.5 Vehicle and Mileage Expenses

建议文案：

Travel between suppliers, customers, job sites, equipment rental locations, and waste facilities may create eligible business vehicle expenses.

必须说明：

普通通勤与业务行驶需要区分
个人和业务车辆使用需要记录
用户应保留日期、目的地、距离和业务用途
SnapTax主要整理车辆相关收据
SnapTax不能替代完整Mileage Log

产品文案：

Use SnapTax to organize fuel, parking, toll, maintenance, and other vehicle-related receipts. Keep a separate mileage log if you plan to use the standard mileage method.
10.6 Equipment Rental

建议文案：

Roofing contractors may rent lifts, trailers, compressors, generators, scaffolding, disposal containers, and other equipment for specific projects.

记录建议：

Add the project name and rental period to make the business purpose easier to verify later.
10.7 Dump and Disposal Fees

建议文案：

Roof removal can create disposal costs such as landfill charges, dump fees, debris hauling, and container rental.

记录建议：

Save the receipt and add the related job or project when possible.

该分类是Roofing页面区别于Electrician、HVAC和Plumber的重要内容。

10.8 Licenses, Permits and Training

建议文案：

Roofing contractors may pay for licenses, local building permits, safety training, manufacturer certification, and continuing education.

记录建议：

Save the receipt together with the license, permit, course, or certification name.
10.9 Insurance

建议文案：

Business-related coverage may include general liability, commercial vehicle insurance, workers’ compensation, tool coverage, and other eligible policies.

风险提示：

Personal and business insurance must be distinguished, and eligibility depends on the policy and business structure.

15. FAQ模块
FAQ 1：What can roofers deduct on their taxes?
Potential business expenses may include qualifying roofing materials, tools, safety equipment, vehicle costs, rentals, disposal fees, licenses, permits, insurance, software, advertising, and professional services. Eligibility depends on the nature and business use of each expense.
FAQ 2：Can roofers write off tools and equipment?
Tools and equipment purchased for roofing work may qualify as business expenses. The tax treatment can depend on the cost, expected useful life, and business-use percentage.
FAQ 3：Can roofing materials be deducted?
Shingles, underlayment, flashing, fasteners, sealants, membranes, and other materials purchased for roofing jobs may qualify as business expenses when properly documented.
FAQ 4：Can roofers deduct safety equipment?
Safety harnesses, anchors, helmets, gloves, eye protection, respirators, and other protective equipment required for roofing work may qualify, depending on the circumstances.
FAQ 5：Can I deduct my roofing work truck?
Eligible business vehicle expenses may be deductible, but personal commuting and business travel must be distinguished. Keep records supporting the vehicle’s business use.
FAQ 6：Can roofers deduct dump fees?
Dump, landfill, debris hauling, and related disposal fees paid for roofing projects may qualify as business expenses when supported by appropriate records.
FAQ 7：Can I deduct rented roofing equipment?
Equipment rental costs related to roofing projects may qualify as business expenses. Keep the invoice, rental period, payment record, and related project information.
FAQ 8：Does SnapTax track mileage?
SnapTax can organize vehicle-related receipts, but it does not currently replace a complete mileage log. Keep separate records of each trip’s date, destination, distance, and business purpose.
FAQ 9：Does SnapTax file taxes?
No. SnapTax helps organize receipts and expenses and creates tax-ready reports. It does not prepare or file tax returns.
FAQ 10：Can I give my report to an accountant?
Yes. You can export an organized expense report and use it when reviewing your records with an accountant or tax preparer.
