1. 项目背景

SnapTax 是一款面向美国 1099 工作者、独立承包商和小型企业的极简收据及费用管理工具，核心能力包括：

AI 识别商户、日期和金额
根据 IRS Schedule C 相关类别整理费用
标记识别不确定的收据
追踪年度业务支出
生成 tax-ready expense report
支持移动端及离线使用
无需强制注册即可开始
一次性税季付费，无订阅

Plumber 日常工作具有明显的移动化、现场化和费用碎片化特点。用户通常在供应商、五金店、加油站和不同客户现场之间移动，并频繁购买：

阀门和水龙头
密封材料
排水清理工具
电动工具
防护用品
车辆燃油
许可证和培训
广告及业务软件

大量纸质收据容易散落在服务车辆、工具箱、钱包或工作服口袋中。到了税务季，用户需要重新查看银行账单、邮箱和纸质收据，难以准确回忆每笔费用的业务用途。

SnapTax 可以在费用发生时完成数字化记录，降低年底集中整理的成本。

1. 重点扣除分类说明

10.1 Tools & Equipment

建议文案：

Plumbers often purchase pipe wrenches, cutters, inspection cameras, press tools, power tools, leak detectors, soldering equipment, and drain-cleaning tools.

记录建议：

Save the receipt, purchase date, cost, and a note explaining how the tool is used in your plumbing business.

风险提示：

The tax treatment may depend on the cost, useful life, and business-use percentage of the equipment. Some equipment may need to be depreciated.
10.2 Parts & Supplies

建议文案：

Pipes, fittings, valves, faucets, connectors, traps, repair kits, sealants, solder, and other job supplies may qualify as business expenses when purchased for plumbing work.

记录建议：

Add the customer, job, or service type when the materials relate to a specific project.

建议 Job Note：

Water heater replacement — Oak Street
Kitchen leak repair — Customer 204
10.3 Drain Cleaning Equipment

建议文案：

Drain augers, snakes, cables, cutters, jetting accessories, inspection equipment, and related maintenance supplies may be business expenses when used for plumbing services.

记录建议：

Separate larger equipment purchases from disposable supplies and routine replacement parts.
10.4 Vehicle and Mileage Expenses

建议文案：

Travel between customers, suppliers, job sites, and other business destinations may create eligible vehicle expenses.

必须说明：

家庭到固定工作地点的普通通勤不等同于业务里程
业务使用和个人使用需要区分
用户应保留日期、目的地、距离及业务用途
SnapTax 当前主要整理车辆相关收据
如果没有完整里程功能，不得宣称自动追踪里程

产品文案：

Use SnapTax to organize fuel, parking, toll, maintenance, and other vehicle-related receipts. Keep a separate mileage log if you use the standard mileage method.
10.5 Licenses, Permits and Training

建议文案：

Plumbers may pay for license renewals, local permits, code training, safety courses, continuing education, and professional certifications.

记录建议：

Save the payment receipt together with the license, permit, course, or certification name.
10.6 Safety Gear and Work Clothing

建议文案：

Protective gloves, safety glasses, respirators, knee pads, hard hats, and specialized protective clothing required for plumbing work may qualify as business expenses.

风险提示：

Ordinary clothing that can be worn outside work may be treated differently.
10.7 Phone, Software and Business Services

建议文案：

Plumbing contractors often use phones, scheduling tools, invoicing software, cloud storage, websites, and other services to operate their businesses.

风险提示：

When a service has both personal and business use, keep a record of the business-use portion.

15. FAQ 模块
FAQ 1：What can plumbers deduct on their taxes?
Potential business expenses may include qualifying tools, equipment, parts, supplies, safety gear, licenses, permits, training, insurance, vehicle expenses, software, advertising, and professional services. Eligibility depends on the nature and business use of each expense.
FAQ 2：Can plumbers write off tools?
Tools purchased for plumbing work may qualify as business expenses. The tax treatment can depend on the cost, useful life, and business-use percentage of the tool.
FAQ 3：Can plumbing materials be deducted?
Pipes, fittings, valves, connectors, sealants, repair parts, and other materials purchased for plumbing jobs may qualify as business expenses when properly documented.
FAQ 4：Can I deduct my plumbing service truck?
Eligible business vehicle expenses may be deductible, but personal commuting and business travel must be distinguished. Keep records supporting the business use of the vehicle.
FAQ 5：Can plumbers deduct work clothes?
Specialized protective clothing and safety equipment required for plumbing work may qualify. Ordinary clothing that can be worn outside work is generally treated differently.
FAQ 6：Can I deduct plumbing licenses and permits?
License renewals, eligible permits, professional fees, and continuing education related to an existing plumbing business may qualify, depending on the circumstances.
FAQ 7：Does SnapTax track mileage?

如果产品尚未提供完整里程功能：

SnapTax can organize vehicle-related receipts, but it does not currently replace a complete mileage log. Keep separate records of each trip’s date, destination, distance, and business purpose.
FAQ 8：Does SnapTax file taxes?
No. SnapTax helps organize receipts and expenses and creates tax-ready reports. It does not prepare or file tax returns.
FAQ 9：Can I give my report to an accountant?
Yes. You can export an organized expense report and use it when reviewing your records with an accountant or tax preparer.
FAQ 10：Is SnapTax accounting software?
SnapTax is focused on receipt capture, expense organization, and tax-season reporting. It is not a full bookkeeping or accounting platform.
